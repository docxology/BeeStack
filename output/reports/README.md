# Output Reports

Generated markdown and text reports for BeeStack analysis runs.
`bee_visual_verification.md` records actual GIF-frame and MJCF evidence that
the FlyBody walking and flight renders carry honeybee visual and locomotion cues
instead of stock fly or static-sprite cues, and now also checks that BeeSwarm
waggle/collision production GIFs are strict MuJoCo/FlyBody scene renders.
`flybody_contact_physics.md` and `.json` record scene XML paths, frame counts,
bee-bee contact pairs, floor contacts, minimum contact distance, and body-plan
provenance for those BeeSwarm scenes.
`beestack_integrity_review.md` and `.json` summarize public APIs, cross-layer
contracts, validation checks, diagnostics, empirical evidence, fidelity levels,
and known gaps for Body, Brain, Mind, Swarm, and Niche.
`beestack_research_report.md` and `.json` are the central science-first report
with module method scorecards, validation fractions, empirical evidence,
visualization inventory, sensitivity sweeps, and known gaps.
`project_readiness_review.md` and `.json` summarize strict README/AGENTS
coverage, documentation-audit health, research-suite known gaps, and
prioritized project improvements.
`digital_twin_readiness.md` is the generated roadmap assessment for the full
systems-biology colony and population-of-colonies digital-twin target; it
records current evidence tiers, missing capabilities, validation datasets,
required artifacts, and acceptance tests without claiming the target is already
implemented.
`empirical_analysis.md` records downloaded bee-data validation, empirical
template integration, Jernigan antennal active-sensing integration, archive
availability caveats, and stack alignment outputs.
`documentation_audit.md` records documentation freshness, command references,
source links, generated-output references, unresolved manuscript variables, and
fidelity-language coverage.
