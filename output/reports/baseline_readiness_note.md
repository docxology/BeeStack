# BeeStack Baseline Readiness Note

This snapshot is intended to be tracked under the Full Snapshot policy. It keeps
generated reports, figures, GIFs, manifests, and lightweight JSON/Markdown data
outputs in the project review surface while excluding dependency folders,
caches, coverage files, local PDF/slide/web exports, and raw external empirical
archives.

## Acceptance Targets

- Source coverage: at least 92%.
- BeeBrain empirical readiness: `brain_data_parseable_fraction >= 0.800`, or all
  remaining blockers are source-verified and documented.
- BeeBody calibration: morphology score at or above 0.85.
- BeeSwarm waggle: mean follower orientation error below 35 degrees and
  orientation confidence above 0.65.
- BeeNiche thermal calibration: brood-temperature error below 3 degrees C.
- Documentation readiness: documentation audit and signposting check pass.

## Known Remaining Gaps

Remaining high-fidelity gaps are documented rather than hidden: Paoli calcium
payload access may still be blocked upstream, BeeBody inertial tensors need
biomechanical calibration beyond morphology witnesses, BeeMind transition terms
remain reduced/fitted witnesses, and BeeNiche remains a deterministic comb and
thermal kernel rather than a full hive-environment engine.
